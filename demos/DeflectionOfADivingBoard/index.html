Directions

In this Demonstration, a woman stands on a diving board that is anchored to the wall on the left. Use the sliders to set the mass of the woman, her distance from the left wall, the board width and the board thickness. The deflection from horizontal is shown on the diagram at the location where the woman is standing. Assume that the diving board is weightless, so the length of the board does not affect deflection.

Details

A woman stands on a diving board that is attached to a wall on the left. The diving board has width \(b\) and thickness \(h\). Deflection \(\nu\) of the board from the horizontal position for \(0 \le x \le L\) is:

\(\nu(x) = -\dfrac{m \, g \, x^2}{6 \, E \, I}\,(3 L - x)\),

where \(m\) is the mass of the woman (kg), \(g = 9.8~\mathrm{m}/\mathrm{s}^2\) is acceleration due to gravity, \(x\) is distance from the wall (m), \(L\) is the distance from the wall where the woman is standing (m), \(E\) is the modulus of elasticity for plastic (Pa), and \(I = \frac{1}{12} b h^3\) is the moment of inertia (\(\mathrm{m}^4\)).

For \(x > L\), the deflection of the board is:

\(\nu(x) = \nu_{max} - \dfrac{m \, g \, L^2}{2 \, E \, I}\,(x - L)\),

where \(\nu_{max} = -\dfrac{m \, g \, L^3}{3 \, E \, I}\) is maximum deflection.

Symbols

\(b\)board width (m)
\(E\)modulus of elasticity of plastic, \(3 \times 10^9\) Pa
\(g\)acceleration due to gravity, \(9.8~\mathrm{m}/\mathrm{s}^2\)
\(h\)board thickness (m)
\(I\)moment of inertia of the board cross section (\(\mathrm{m}^4\))
\(L\)distance from the wall where the woman stands (m)
\(m\)mass of the woman (kg)
\(x\)distance from the wall along the board (m)
\(\nu\)deflection of the board from horizontal (m)
\(\nu_{max}\)maximum deflection, at \(x = L\) (m)
