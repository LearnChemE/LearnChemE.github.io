Adiabatic Evaporation of Water into Vacuum

Directions

In this simulation, a cylinder fitted with a weightless, frictionless piston initially contains a liquid slightly above its saturation pressure; the volume above the piston is initially under vacuum. You can select the initial mass and temperature of the liquid using the sliders. The final volume is fixed at 1.0 L. Expansion is enabled when the red stop is removed. The piston then moves to the top of the cylinder as some liquid evaporates, resulting in the final values of temperature, pressure, and liquid mass, as shown. The temperature drops due to evaporative cooling, while the final pressure reaches its saturation value. Use the “reset” button to start again. The liquid volume in the cylinder is exaggerated relative to the vapor volume for better visualization.
