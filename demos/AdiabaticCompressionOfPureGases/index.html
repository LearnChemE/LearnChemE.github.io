Directions

In this simulation, n-butane is compressed adiabatically from 1 bar and 50°C to user-set values for the discharge stream pressure and the compressor efficiency η. Select “sketch of apparatus” to determine the outlet stream temperature (expressed in °C) and the power consumed (expressed in kW). The inlet molar flow rate of n-butane supplied to the compressor is ṅ = 1 mol/s. You can select from one of three cubic equations of state (Redlich–Kwong, Soave–Redlich–Kwong (SRK), or Peng–Robinson). An ideal gas at 1 bar and 298.15 K is taken as the reference state for computing the enthalpy H and entropy S for the system flow streams. In the temperature–entropy (T–S) diagram, the blue curve denotes the inlet stream isobar (P = 1 bar) and the red curve the discharge stream isobar. A limiting compression transformation on the T–S diagram is shown in dashed green. You can clearly see the difference between the ideal case (shown by a vertical dashed green line), when the compressor is isentropic (i.e., η = 100%), and a more realistic case, where the efficiency is less than 100% (shown by a tilted dashed green line).

Details

n-Butane (Tc = 425.1 K, Pc = 37.96 bar, ω = 0.200) is compressed from state 1 (inlet, 1 bar, 50°C) to state 2 (outlet, at the discharge pressure set by the slider). Results agree closely with a commercial process simulator (HYSYS) for the SRK and Peng–Robinson equations of state. Other pure gases can be studied by changing the critical temperature Tc, critical pressure Pc, and acentric factor ω.

Definitions of symbols

  • T, P — temperature (K) and pressure (bar) of a stream
  • Tc, Pc, ω — critical temperature, critical pressure, and acentric factor
  • Tr = T/Tc, Pr = P/Pc — reduced temperature and pressure
  • Z — compressibility factor, PV/RT
  • R — universal gas constant, 8.314 J/(mol·K)
  • β, q, α(Tr), ε, σ, Ω, Ψ — generalized cubic-equation-of-state parameters
  • HR, SR — residual molar enthalpy and entropy (real gas − ideal gas, same T and P)
  • ΔHig, ΔSig — ideal-gas molar enthalpy and entropy change from the reference state
  • H, S — total molar enthalpy and entropy
  • T1, P1 — inlet temperature (323.15 K) and pressure (1 bar)
  • T2, P2 — actual outlet temperature and discharge pressure (slider)
  • T2s — outlet temperature for an isentropic (100% efficient) compressor
  • η — compressor (isentropic) efficiency
  • ṅ — molar flow rate, 1 mol/s
  • Ẇs — shaft power consumed by the compressor
