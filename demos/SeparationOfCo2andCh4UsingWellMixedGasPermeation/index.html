Directions

A mixture of carbon dioxide and methane are to be separated by a cellulose acetate membrane at 35°C. The permeability of carbon dioxide and methane are 15.0×10−10 and 0.48×10−10 cm3(STP)·cm/(cm2·s·cmHg), respectively, so the selectivity is αCO2−CH4 = 31. The high (retentate side) and low (permeate side) pressures are 20.0 and 1.1 atm, respectively. The plot shows (1) the RT curve, which relates the CO2 mole fractions on both sides of the membrane, and (2) the operating line, whose slope depends on the stage cut, θ; assuming that both the retentate and permeate sides are well mixed, the graphical solution of this gas permeation problem is given by the intersection point shown in cyan in the figure. Use the sliders to set the value of the feed composition (mole fraction of CO2) as well as the stage cut.

Details

For a well-mixed permeator, the CO2 mole fraction on the permeate side, $y_p$, is related to the mole fraction on the retentate side, $y_r$, through the RT curve:

\[ y_p\Big[(\alpha-1)\frac{p_p}{p_r}(1-y_p)+1\Big] = y_r\big[\alpha-(\alpha-1)y_p\big] \]

where $\alpha$ is the membrane selectivity and $p_p$ and $p_r$ are the permeate- and retentate-side pressures:

\[ \alpha = \frac{P_{CO_2}}{P_{CH_4}} = \frac{15.0\times10^{-10}}{0.48\times10^{-10}} = 31.25 \]

An overall mole balance on CO2 around the membrane module, in terms of the feed composition $y_{in}$ and the stage cut $\theta$ (the fraction of the feed that permeates), gives the operating line:

\[ y_{in} = \theta\,y_p + (1-\theta)\,y_r \qquad \Longrightarrow \qquad y_p = \frac{y_{in}}{\theta} - \frac{1-\theta}{\theta}\,y_r \]

The operating line is plotted for $0\le y_r\le y_{in}$. The simultaneous solution of the RT-curve equation and the operating-line equation, found numerically, gives the operating point (shown as the cyan point), from which the retentate and permeate compositions are read.

Parameter values used: $p_r = 20.0$ atm, $p_p = 1.1$ atm, $P_{CO_2} = 15.0\times10^{-10}$, $P_{CH_4} = 0.48\times10^{-10}$ cm3(STP)·cm/(cm2·s·cmHg).

Symbols

  • $y_{in}$ = mole fraction of CO2 in the feed
  • $y_r$ = mole fraction of CO2 on the retentate (high-pressure) side
  • $y_p$ = mole fraction of CO2 on the permeate (low-pressure) side
  • $\theta$ = stage cut (fraction of the feed that permeates the membrane)
  • $\alpha$ = membrane selectivity for CO2 relative to CH4
  • $p_r$ = retentate-side pressure (atm)
  • $p_p$ = permeate-side pressure (atm)
  • $P_{CO_2}$, $P_{CH_4}$ = permeabilities of CO2 and CH4 in the membrane (cm3(STP)·cm/(cm2·s·cmHg))
