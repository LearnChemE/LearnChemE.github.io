Directions

In this simulation, hot and cold water are injected into insulated tanks containing liquid nitrogen. The volume of water injected has a large impact on the amount of liquid nitrogen evaporated. Adjust the sliders to change the volume and temperature of water in each syringe, then click the play button. After equilibrium is reached, select the reset button to run the simulation for different starting conditions.

The simulation may be paused at any time, and the animation speed may be increased or decreased using the up arrow and down arrow buttons. The simulation is at a much slower speed than the actual process in order to help visualize the process, and the volume of the ice cubes is exaggerated relative to the volume of the nitrogen gas.

Details

In an adiabatic system with excess liquid nitrogen, the mass of nitrogen vaporized \(m_{\text{vap}}\) depends on the mass of water injected \(m_{w}\), the specific enthalpy of the injected water \(\hat H_{w,\,i}\) and the final enthalpy \(\hat H_{w,\,f}\) of the water, which at equilibrium is the enthalpy of ice at \(-196\ ^{\circ}\text{C}\):

\[ m_{\text{vap}}\,\Delta \hat H^{\text{vap}}_{\text{N}_2} = m_{w}\left(\hat H_{w,\,i}-\hat H_{w,\,f}\right), \]

where \(\Delta \hat H^{\text{vap}}_{\text{N}_2}\) is the specific enthalpy of vaporization of nitrogen.

The difference between the initial and final specific enthalpies of water is the sum of the sensible heat changes and the heat of fusion of water \(\left(\Delta \hat H^{\text{melt}}_{\text{H}_2\text{O}}\right)\):

\[ \hat H_{w,\,i}-\hat H_{w,\,f} = c_{p,\,\text{liq}}\left(T_{\text{init}}-T_{\text{melt}}\right) + \Delta \hat H^{\text{melt}}_{\text{H}_2\text{O}} + c_{p,\,\text{ice}}\left(T_{\text{melt}}-T_{\text{final}}\right), \]

where \(c_{p,\,\text{liq}}\) and \(c_{p,\,\text{ice}}\) are the heat capacities of liquid water and ice, respectively, \(T_{\text{init}}\) is the initial temperature of the water, \(T_{\text{melt}}\) is the freezing temperature of water \((0\ ^{\circ}\text{C})\), and \(T_{\text{final}}\) is \(-196\ ^{\circ}\text{C}\), the boiling temperature of liquid nitrogen.

Values used in this simulation (per mole): \(c_{p,\,\text{liq}} = 75.3\ \text{J/(mol·K)}\), \(\Delta \hat H^{\text{melt}}_{\text{H}_2\text{O}} = 6002\ \text{J/mol}\), \(c_{p,\,\text{ice}} = 37.816 + 0.1283\,T\ \text{J/(mol·K)}\) (with \(T\) in \(^{\circ}\text{C}\)), and \(\Delta \hat H^{\text{vap}}_{\text{N}_2} = 6100\ \text{J/mol}\). The density of nitrogen vapor is taken as \(1.25\ \text{g/L}\).

Symbols

\(c_{p,\,\text{ice}}\) = heat capacity of ice (J/(mol·K))
\(c_{p,\,\text{liq}}\) = heat capacity of liquid water (J/(mol·K))
\(\hat H_{w,\,f}\) = specific enthalpy of the water at equilibrium (ice at \(T_{\text{final}}\))
\(\hat H_{w,\,i}\) = specific enthalpy of the injected water
\(m_{\text{vap}}\) = mass of nitrogen vaporized
\(m_{w}\) = mass of water injected
\(T\) = temperature in the ice heat-capacity correlation (\(^{\circ}\text{C}\))
\(T_{\text{final}}\) = final temperature of the water, the boiling temperature of liquid nitrogen (\(-196\ ^{\circ}\text{C}\))
\(T_{\text{init}}\) = initial temperature of the injected water (\(^{\circ}\text{C}\))
\(T_{\text{melt}}\) = freezing temperature of water (\(0\ ^{\circ}\text{C}\))
\(\Delta \hat H^{\text{melt}}_{\text{H}_2\text{O}}\) = heat of fusion of water (J/mol)
\(\Delta \hat H^{\text{vap}}_{\text{N}_2}\) = heat of vaporization of nitrogen (J/mol)
