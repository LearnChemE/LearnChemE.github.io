Directions

This simulation calculates radiative heat transfer from the green surface to the black surface as a function of the temperature of the green surface. Both surfaces are blackbodies which lose energy by emission, but absorb all incident radiation. The blackbodies are maintained at constant temperatures by internal heaters. You can select “parallel plates”, “perpendicular walls” or “parallel cylinders”. The view factor \(F_{ij}\) is shown. The rate that heat transfers from the green surface and is intercepted by the black surface is plotted as a function of the temperature of the green surface. The magenta point on the plot represents the green surface temperature (select with a slider). You can change distances between surfaces and dimensions of the plates, walls or cylinders.

Details

The view factor \(F_{ij}\) is defined as the fraction of the radiation leaving the green surface i that is intercepted by the black surface j. The view factors for each situation are shown below.

Parallel plates:

$$\begin{aligned}F_{ij}=\frac{2}{\pi\,\bar{X}\,\bar{Y}}\Bigg(&\ln\sqrt{\frac{\left(1+\bar{X}^{2}\right)\left(1+\bar{Y}^{2}\right)}{1+\bar{X}^{2}+\bar{Y}^{2}}} +\bar{X}\sqrt{1+\bar{Y}^{2}}\,\tan^{-1}\!\left(\frac{\bar{X}}{\sqrt{1+\bar{Y}^{2}}}\right)\\ &+\bar{Y}\sqrt{1+\bar{X}^{2}}\,\tan^{-1}\!\left(\frac{\bar{Y}}{\sqrt{1+\bar{X}^{2}}}\right) -\bar{X}\tan^{-1}\bar{X}-\bar{Y}\tan^{-1}\bar{Y}\Bigg),\end{aligned}$$

$$\bar{X}=\frac{X}{L},$$

$$\bar{Y}=\frac{Y}{L},$$

where \(X\) is the plate length, \(Y\) is the plate width and \(L\) is the distance between the plates.

Perpendicular walls:

$$F_{ij}=\frac{1+X/Y-\sqrt{1+\left(X/Y\right)^{2}}}{2},$$

where \(X\) and \(Y\) are the lengths of the walls.

Parallel cylinders:

$$\begin{aligned}F_{ij}=\frac{1}{2\pi}\Bigg(&\pi+\sqrt{C^{2}-\left(R+1\right)^{2}}-\sqrt{C^{2}-\left(R-1\right)^{2}}\\ &+\left(R-1\right)\cos^{-1}\!\left(\frac{R}{C}-\frac{1}{C}\right) -\left(R+1\right)\cos^{-1}\!\left(\frac{R}{C}+\frac{1}{C}\right)\Bigg),\end{aligned}$$

$$R=\frac{r_{\mathrm{j}}}{r_{\mathrm{i}}},$$

$$S=\frac{s}{r_{\mathrm{i}}},$$

$$C=1+R+S,$$

where \(r\) is the cylinder radius and \(s\) is the space between the cylinders.

The rate of heat transfer from surface \(\mathrm{i}\) to \(\mathrm{j}\) is:

$$q_{\mathrm{i}}=A_{\mathrm{i}}F_{ij}\sigma\left(T_{\mathrm{i}}^{4}-T_{\mathrm{j}}^{4}\right),$$

where \(A_{\mathrm{i}}\) is the area of surface \(\mathrm{i}\), \(\sigma\times 10^{-4}=5.67\times 10^{-12}\) is the Stefan–Boltzmann constant and \(T\) is surface temperature.

Reference

[1] T. L. Bergman, A. S. Lavine, F. P. Incropera and D. P. DeWitt, Introduction to Heat Transfer, 6th ed., Hoboken: John Wiley and Sons, 2011 pp. 832–833, 839.
