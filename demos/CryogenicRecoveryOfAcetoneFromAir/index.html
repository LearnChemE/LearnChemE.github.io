Directions

A 5 mol% acetone/95 mole% air stream flows into a condenser at a rate of 1000 kmol/h. The acetone is recovered from air using a cryogenic process with temperatures between −100 °C and −20 °C. The liquid phase that exits the process is pure acetone. A 10 °C approach temperature is chosen, which allows the determination of the isothermal flash temperature from the user-set value of the coolant temperature. The simulation displays the flow rates and their mole fractions. At sufficiently low condenser temperatures, almost 100% of the acetone can be recovered.

Details

The feed (component 1 = acetone, component 2 = air) is flashed at the drum temperature \(T\), which is set by a 10 °C approach to the coolant temperature \(\theta\):

$$T = \theta + 10 + 273.15\;\text{K}$$

The overall and acetone mole balances on the flash drum are

$$F = L + V, \qquad F\,z = L\,x + V\,y,$$

with feed rate \(F = 1000\ \text{kmol/h}\) and feed acetone fraction \(z = 0.05\). Here \(x\) and \(y\) are the acetone mole fractions in the liquid and vapor, \(L\) and \(V\) are the liquid and vapor molar flow rates.

Vapor–liquid equilibrium uses a modified Raoult's law with Wilson activity coefficients \(\gamma_i\):

$$y = \frac{\gamma_1\,P_1^{\,\text{sat}}\,x}{P}, \qquad 1 - y = \frac{\gamma_2\,P_2^{\,\text{sat}}\,(1-x)}{P},$$

with total pressure \(P = 101.325\ \text{kPa}\).

The saturation pressures (kPa) are given by the extended (DIPPR) Antoine form:

$$\ln P_i^{\,\text{sat}} = A_i + \frac{B_i}{C_i + T} + D_i \ln T + E_i\,T^{\,F_i}$$

The Wilson model gives

$$\Lambda_{12} = \frac{V_2}{V_1}\, \exp\!\left(-\frac{\lambda_{12} + b_{12}\,T}{R\,T}\right),$$
$$\Lambda_{21} = \frac{V_1}{V_2}\, \exp\!\left(-\frac{\lambda_{21} + b_{21}\,T}{R\,T}\right),$$
$$\ln\gamma_1 = -\ln\!\left(x_1 + x_2\Lambda_{12}\right) + 1 - \left[\frac{x_1}{x_1 + x_2\Lambda_{12}} + \frac{x_2\,\Lambda_{21}}{x_1\Lambda_{21} + x_2}\right],$$
$$\ln\gamma_2 = -\ln\!\left(x_1\Lambda_{21} + x_2\right) + 1 - \left[\frac{x_1\,\Lambda_{12}}{x_1 + x_2\Lambda_{12}} + \frac{x_2}{x_1\Lambda_{21} + x_2}\right],$$

where \(x_1 = x\), \(x_2 = 1 - x\), and \(R = 1.987\ \text{cal/(mol·K)}\). The four VLE and balance equations are solved for \(x,\,y,\,L,\,V\) at each temperature.
