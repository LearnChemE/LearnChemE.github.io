Directions

This simulation shows how the reaction stoichiometry affects the volumetric flow rate in an isothermal, constant-pressure, plug flow reactor by following a control volume (initially 10 mL of A) through the reactor. In the gas phase reaction A → m B, the reactant A is blue and the product B is green. Use the buttons to change the reaction stoichiometry m and the reaction order. Use the slider to change the reactor pressure. The rate constants are chosen so that the initial rates are the same for all orders at 10-bar pressure. The inlet velocity is fixed at 1.4 cm/s, independent of pressure, so the feed molar flow rates change when pressure changes. Click the "Play" button to run the reaction and click the "Reset" button to start over. The volumetric flow rate is shown below the reactor, and the change in the 10-mL inlet volume is shown above the reactor.

Details

The molar flow rate for species \(i = (\mathrm{A}, \mathrm{B})\) through the reactor is related to the volumetric flow rate using the ideal gas law:

\[ F_i = y_i \, \frac{P \, v}{R \, T}, \]

\[ F_\mathrm{T} = \frac{P \, v}{R \, T}, \]

where \(F_\mathrm{T}\) is the total molar flow rate, \(y_i\) is the mole fraction, \(P\) is reactor pressure, and \(v\) is the volumetric flow rate.

Mass balances are used to calculate the molar flow rates of A and B down the reactor:

\[ \frac{dF_\mathrm{A}}{dz} = -A \, k \, C_\mathrm{A}^{\,n}, \]

\[ \frac{dF_\mathrm{B}}{dz} = m \, A \, k \, C_\mathrm{A}^{\,n}, \]

where \(n\) is the reaction order, \(m\) is the moles of B formed for each mole of A reacted, \(z\) is distance down the reactor, \(A\) is the cross-sectional area of the reactor, and \(C_i\) is concentration. The rate constants are chosen so the rate at the reactor inlet is the same for each reaction order at a pressure of 10 bar.

The concentration of A is related to molar flow rates using the ideal gas law:

\[ C_\mathrm{A} = \frac{F_\mathrm{A}}{F_\mathrm{A} + F_\mathrm{B}} \, \frac{P}{R \, T} = y_\mathrm{A} \, \frac{P}{R \, T}. \]

The conversion of A is:

\[ X_\mathrm{A} = \frac{F_{\mathrm{A},0} - F_\mathrm{A}} {F_{\mathrm{A},0}}, \]

where \(F_{\mathrm{A},0}\) is the inlet molar flow rate of A.

The change in the control volume is the control volume at any location minus the initial control volume (\(V_0 = 10\) mL):

\[ \Delta V = V - V_0. \]

Symbols

  • \(A\) = reactor cross-sectional area (cm2)
  • \(C_i\) = concentration of species \(i\) (mol/cm3)
  • \(F_i\) = molar flow rate of species \(i\) (mol/s)
  • \(F_{\mathrm{A},0}\) = inlet molar flow rate of A (mol/s)
  • \(F_\mathrm{T}\) = total molar flow rate (mol/s)
  • \(k\) = rate constant (units depend on reaction order)
  • \(m\) = moles of B formed per mole of A reacted
  • \(n\) = reaction order
  • \(P\) = reactor pressure (bar)
  • \(R\) = ideal gas constant (83.14 cm3 bar/(mol K))
  • \(T\) = temperature (650 K)
  • \(v\) = volumetric flow rate (mL/s)
  • \(V\) = control volume (mL)
  • \(V_0\) = initial control volume (10 mL)
  • \(\Delta V\) = change in control volume (mL)
  • \(X_\mathrm{A}\) = conversion of A
  • \(y_i\) = mole fraction of species \(i\)
  • \(z\) = distance down the reactor (cm)
