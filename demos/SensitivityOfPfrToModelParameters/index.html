Plots of temperature and molar flow rates versus reactor volume are shown for gas-phase reactions A → B → C in a non-isothermal, plug flow reactor (PFR). Both reactions are first order and only A is fed to the reactor. Use the sliders to vary the inlet molar flow rate, feed temperature and seven model parameters (a maximum of 10%) to see how sensitive the temperature and molar flow rates are to values used in the reactor model (parametric sensitivity). Since parameters used in a reactor model are not always known accurately, doing a parametric sensitivity analysis could identify potential problems.

Details

The rate laws for these reactions are:

\[ r_1 = -k_1 C_A, \]

\[ r_2 = -k_2 C_B, \]

\[ C_A = C_{A,\,0}\, \frac{F_A}{F_T}\, \frac{T_0}{T}, \]

\[ C_B = C_{A,\,0}\, \frac{F_B}{F_T}\, \frac{T_0}{T}, \]

\[ F_T = F_A + F_B + F_C, \]

where \(r_i\) is the rate of reaction \(i = (1, 2)\) (mol/(dm\(^3\) s)), \(k_i\) is the rate constant (1/s), \(T\) is reaction temperature (K), \(C_A\) and \(C_B\) are the concentrations of A and B (mol/dm\(^3\)), the subscript \(0\) refers to the inlet, \(F_A\), \(F_B\) and \(F_C\) are the molar flow rates of A, B and C (mol/s) and \(F_T\) is the total molar flow rate (mol/s).

The rate constants are calculated from the pre-exponential factors which are solved at \(T = 380\) K:

\[ A_i = \frac{k_{s,\,i}}{e^{-\frac{Ea_i}{R\,380}}}, \]

\[ k_i = A_i\, e^{-\frac{Ea_i}{R\,T}}, \]

where \(A_i\) is a pre-exponential factor (1/s), \(k_{s,\,i}\) is the rate constant at 380 K, \(Ea_i\) is activation energy (kJ/mol) and \(R\) is the ideal gas constant (kJ/(mol K)),

Mole and energy balances are done around the reactor:

\[ \frac{d F_A}{d V} = r_1, \]

\[ \frac{d F_B}{d V} = -r_1 + r_2, \]

\[ \frac{d F_C}{d V} = -r_2, \]

\[ \frac{d T}{d V} = \frac{Ua\,(T_a - T) + r_1\, \Delta H_1 + r_2\, \Delta H_2}{F_A\, Cp_A + F_B\, Cp_B + F_C\, Cp_C}, \]

where \(V\) is volume (m\(^3\)), \(Ua\) is the heat transfer coefficient times heat transfer area per volume (kJ/(m\(^3\) s K)), \(T_a\) is the coolant temperature (K), \(\Delta H_i\) is heat of reaction (kJ/mol), and \(Cp_A\), \(Cp_B\) and \(Cp_C\) are the heat capacities of A, B and C (kJ/(mol K)).
