Directions

This simulation calculates the tension required to pull a cart with an acceleration that you vary with a slider. Force balances and Newton's second law of motion are used to calculate the tension, normal force, and force due to friction. Use the sliders to change the angle of the cable used to pull the cart, the mass of the cart, and the coefficient of friction.

Details

Force balances in the \(x\) and \(y\) directions:

$$\sum F_x = m\,a = F_T\cos\theta - F_F,$$
$$\sum F_y = 0 = F_N + F_T\sin\theta - m\,g,$$

where the force due to friction is

$$F_F = \mu F_N.$$

Symbols:

  • \(a\) = acceleration of the cart (m/s\(^2\))
  • \(F_F\) = force due to friction (N)
  • \(F_N\) = normal force pushing up on the cart (N)
  • \(F_T\) = force of tension (N)
  • \(F_x, F_y\) = forces in the \(x\) and \(y\) directions (N)
  • \(g\) = acceleration due to gravity, 9.8 m/s\(^2\)
  • \(m\) = mass of the cart (kg)
  • \(\theta\) = angle of elevation of the rope pulling the cart (degrees)
  • \(\mu\) = coefficient of friction
