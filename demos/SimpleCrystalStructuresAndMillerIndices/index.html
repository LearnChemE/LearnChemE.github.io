Directions

This simulation illustrates several crystal structures, showing unit cells and Miller indices.

Details

Differences in simple cubic, body-centered cubic, face-centered cubic, and hexagonal close packed structures are shown. Also illustrated are structures for CsCl, diamond, NaCl, and ZnS.

Planes are drawn for Miller indices that are selected.
