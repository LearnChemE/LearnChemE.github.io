Directions

Explore the hydrodynamics of a packed bed of sand. Adjust the fluid and particle properties to calculate the minimum fluidization velocity (umf) using the Ergun equation and Wen and Yu approximations.

  1. Select the fluid (water or air) flowing upward through the bed.
  2. Choose the effective sand particle diameter.
  3. Adjust the sphericity slider to describe how close the particles are to a perfect sphere.
  4. Increase the superficial velocity slider to raise the upward fluid velocity through the bed.
  5. Watch the bed transition from a fixed (stationary) bed to a fluidized bed once the superficial velocity exceeds the minimum fluidization velocity.
  6. Read the calculated values in the calculations panel to the right, including the minimum fluidization velocity, porosity, bed expansion, and particle Reynolds number.

Details

The simulation calculates the minimum fluidization velocity by equating the pressure drop from the Ergun equation to the buoyant weight of the bed, then estimates the porosity at minimum fluidization with the Wen and Yu approximation.

Symbols

  • \(\rho_p\) — particle density
  • \(\rho_f\) — fluid density
  • \(\mu\) — fluid viscosity
  • \(g\) — gravitational acceleration
  • \(D_p\) — effective particle diameter
  • \(\phi_s\) — particle sphericity
  • \(u_0\) — superficial velocity
  • \(\epsilon\) — bed porosity
  • \(\epsilon_{mf}\) — porosity at minimum fluidization
  • \(u_{mf}\) — minimum fluidization velocity
  • \(Re_p\) — particle Reynolds number

Ergun equation (equated to weight)

\( (\rho_p - \rho_f)g = \dfrac{150\mu u_0(1-\epsilon)}{\phi_s^2 D_p^2 \epsilon^3} + \dfrac{1.75\rho_f u_0^2}{\phi_s D_p \epsilon^3} \)

Wen and Yu approximation

\( \dfrac{1}{\phi_s \epsilon_{mf}^3} \approx 14 \implies \epsilon_{mf} = (14\phi_s)^{-1/3} \)

Particle Reynolds number

\( Re_p = \dfrac{\rho_f u_0 D_p}{\mu} \)
