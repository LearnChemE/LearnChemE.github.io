Cable Tension Needed to Support Platform
cable 6.5
person 4.0
mass of person (kg) 62
Directions

A woman is standing on a platform attached on the left and supported by a cable on the right. Use sliders to change the distances from the left of the woman and the cable. Set the mass of the woman with a slider. A moment balance is done to determine the tension force in the cable that keeps the platform from rotating.

Details

A moment balance is done about the left point of the platform:

\( \displaystyle \sum M_{\mathrm{L}} = 0 = z_{\mathrm{c}}\, F_{\mathrm{T}} - z_{\mathrm{w}}\, m\, g , \)

where \( F_{\mathrm{T}} \) is the tension force in the cable (N), \( z_{\mathrm{c}} \) and \( z_{\mathrm{w}} \) are the distances of the cable and the woman from the left of the platform (m), \( m \) is the mass of the woman (kg), and \( g \) is the acceleration due to gravity (\( \mathrm{m}/\mathrm{s}^2 \)).
