Ion exchange column

Directions

This simulation models a fixed-bed ion-exchange column softening hard water: influent Ca2+/Mg2+ ions exchange for Na+ on the resin as the feed flows downward through the bed. Use the five sliders to set the feed concentration of Ca2+ plus Mg2+ (C₀), volumetric flow rate (Q), resin capacity (q₀), resin mass (m), and the Thomas rate constant (kTh); each change recomputes the breakthrough time and resets the clock to t = 0. Press Play to advance time and watch the exhausted (orange) zone move down through the column while the breakthrough curve on the right traces out C/C₀ (outlet concentration of Ca2+ + Mg2+ ions divided by their inlet concentration) at the column outlet, drag the time slider to move to any instant directly, or press Reset to return to t = 0.

Details

The effluent concentration of Ca2+ plus Mg2+ ions (C) divided by the feed concentration of these ions (C₀) is modeled with the Thomas equation, the standard analytical model for a favorable, second-order-reversible ion-exchange (or adsorption) equilibrium in a fixed bed:

\[ \frac{C}{C_0} \;=\; \frac{1}{1+\exp\!\Big[\dfrac{k_{Th}}{Q}\big(q_0 m - C_0 Q t\big)\Big]} \]

This can be rewritten using the stoichiometric (ideal) breakthrough time \(t_b\) and a dimensionless sharpness factor \(s\):

\[ t_b=\frac{q_0\,m}{C_0\,Q}, \qquad s=\frac{k_{Th}\,q_0\,m}{Q}=k_{Th}C_0t_b, \qquad \frac{C}{C_0}=\frac{1}{1+\exp\!\big[s\,(1-t/t_b)\big]} \]

\(t_b\) is the time at which C/C₀ = 0.5 (the midpoint of the S-shaped curve); it is also the time an infinitely sharp front would take to exhaust the whole bed. Larger \(s\) (faster mass transfer, i.e. larger \(k_{Th}\)) gives a narrower mass-transfer zone and a steeper breakthrough curve; smaller \(s\) spreads the curve out.

The column diagram extends the same equation along the bed length assuming the mass-transfer zone travels as a self-sharpening ("constant pattern") wave. With \(z\) the fractional bed depth (0 = inlet, 1 = outlet), the local resin saturation is

\[ \theta(z,t)=\frac{1}{1+\exp\!\big[s\,(z-t/t_b)\big]} \]

which reduces to the effluent equation above at \(z=1\).

\(C_0\)feed (influent) hardness concentration of Ca2+ plus Mg2+ ions, meq/L
\(C\)effluent (outlet) concentration of Ca2+ plus Mg2+ ions at time t, meq/L
\(Q\)volumetric flow rate through the bed, L/min
\(q_0\)resin exchange capacity, meq/g resin
\(m\)mass of resin in the bed, g
\(k_{Th}\)Thomas rate constant, L/(meq·min)
\(t_b\)stoichiometric (ideal) breakthrough time, the time at which C/C₀ = 0.5, min
\(s\)dimensionless sharpness factor; larger \(s\) gives a narrower mass-transfer zone and a steeper breakthrough curve
\(z\)fractional bed depth (0 = inlet, 1 = outlet), dimensionless
