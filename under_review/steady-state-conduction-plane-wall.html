Directions

This simulation solves the heat equation in a 1-D, plane wall in the steady state. The boundary condition on the right side of the wall is convection with ambient fluid temperature T∞,R and convection coefficient hR. The boundary condition on the left side of the wall can be chosen with the radio buttons. Options include constant surface temperature at Ts,L, constant surface heat flux at qs″, and convection with ambient fluid temperature T∞,L and convection coefficient hL. Adjust all parameters with the respective slider bar and observe the change in the spatial temperature (left plot) and heat flux (right plot) profiles. Note that the uniform volumetric heat generation q̇ and the thermal conductivity of the wall k can also be adjusted. To simulate a constant surface temperature on the right hand boundary, increase hR to infinity (>10,000 W/m2K).

Details

The steady-state heat equation for a plane wall of thickness \(L\) with uniform volumetric heat generation \(\dot{q}\) and constant thermal conductivity \(k\) is:

\[ \frac{d^{2}T}{dx^{2}} + \frac{\dot{q}}{k} = 0 \]

Integrating twice yields the general solution for the temperature profile:

\[ T(x) = -\frac{\dot{q}\,x^{2}}{2k} + C_{1}x + C_{2} \]

and the heat flux is obtained from Fourier's law:

\[ q''(x) = -k\frac{dT}{dx} = \dot{q}\,x - C_{1}k \]

The right-side boundary condition is convection for all cases:

\[ -k\left.\frac{dT}{dx}\right|_{x=L} = h_{R}\left[T(L) - T_{\infty,R}\right] \]

1. Constant surface temperature   \(T(0) = T_{s,L}\):

\[ C_{2} = T_{s,L}, \qquad C_{1} = \frac{\dot{q}L + \dfrac{\dot{q}L^{2}h_{R}}{2k} + h_{R}\left(T_{\infty,R} - C_{2}\right)}{h_{R}L + k} \]

2. Constant surface heat flux   \(q''(0) = q_{s}''\):

\[ C_{1} = -\frac{q_{s}''}{k}, \qquad C_{2} = \frac{\dot{q}L + \dfrac{\dot{q}L^{2}h_{R}}{2k} + h_{R}T_{\infty,R} - C_{1}\left(h_{R}L + k\right)}{h_{R}} \]

3. Convection   \(-k\left.\dfrac{dT}{dx}\right|_{x=0} = h_{L}\left[T_{\infty,L} - T(0)\right]\):

\[ C_{2} = \frac{\dot{q}L + \dfrac{\dot{q}L^{2}h_{R}}{2k} + h_{R}T_{\infty,R} + \dfrac{h_{L}\left(h_{R}L + k\right)T_{\infty,L}}{k}}{h_{R} + \dfrac{h_{L}\left(h_{R}L + k\right)}{k}}, \qquad C_{1} = -\frac{h_{L}\left(T_{\infty,L} - C_{2}\right)}{k} \]

Symbols:

\(T\) = temperature [K]
\(x\) = position [m]
\(L\) = wall thickness (0.075 m)
\(k\) = thermal conductivity [W/m-K]
\(\dot{q}\) = uniform volumetric heat generation [W/m3]
\(q''\) = heat flux [W/m2]
\(q_{s}''\) = surface heat flux [W/m2]
\(h_{L},\,h_{R}\) = convection coefficients on the left and right surfaces [W/m2-K]
\(T_{\infty,L},\,T_{\infty,R}\) = ambient fluid temperatures on the left and right sides [K]
\(T_{s,L}\) = left surface temperature [K]
\(C_{1},\,C_{2}\) = integration constants
