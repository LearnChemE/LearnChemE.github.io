Catalyst Deactivation in Reactors

Directions

A first-order, irreversible, liquid-phase reaction A → B takes place on a catalyst that loses activity with time on stream. The same mass of catalyst is used in three reactors: a packed-bed reactor (PBR) and a CSTR, in which the catalyst stays in the reactor and deactivates uniformly, and a moving-bed reactor, in which fresh catalyst is fed continuously and spent catalyst is removed.

Select the deactivation order (0, 1, or 2) and use the sliders to set the Damköhler number and the deactivation rate constant at the reference temperature (500 K), the reactor temperature, and the residence time of the catalyst in the moving bed.

The left plot shows catalyst activity versus time; the dashed segment is the average activity of the catalyst in the moving bed. The right plot shows conversion versus time on stream. The moving-bed reactor operates at steady state, so its conversion does not change with time.

Drag on either plot (or use the time-on-stream slider) to move the vertical line; values at that time, and conversions averaged from 0 to that time, are listed below the plots. Use the checkboxes to hide or show reactors and the menu to change the time axis.
