Directions

This simulation calculates the concentration profile and the mass flow rate of n-octane vapor through a stagnant layer of air in a tube in steady-state Fickian diffusion. Pure liquid n-octane is at the bottom of the vertical tube, which has a diameter of 28 cm. Air flows across the open top of the tube to remove n-octane vapor; its concentration at the top is zero. Use sliders to change the total pressure, height of the tube and temperature. The n-octane mole fraction versus tube height curve is shown in blue when convective flux is >10% and in black when it is <10%.
